Yoga

Our yoga classes are designed for all levels, from complete beginners to experienced practitioners. In a warm, supportive environment, you'll learn gentle postures, breathing techniques, and mindfulness practices.

Whether you're looking to improve flexibility, reduce stress, or simply find a quiet moment in your week, our classes offer something for everyone.

Reflexology

Reflexology is a therapeutic technique that applies targeted pressure to specific points on the feet, hands, and ears. These points correspond to different organs and systems in the body.

By stimulating these reflex points, we help promote natural healing, improve circulation, and encourage deep relaxation. Many clients find it particularly beneficial for stress, headaches, and sleep difficulties.

45 - 60 min Deeply relaxing 1-to-1 session

Massage

Our massage therapists offer a range of techniques including Swedish, deep tissue, and aromatherapy massage. Each session is tailored to your individual needs.

Whether you're carrying tension from desk work, recovering from an injury, or simply need time to unwind, therapeutic massage can help ease pain, improve mobility, and restore a sense of calm.
