Our Services

Reflexology, acupuncture, and Swedish massage -holistic therapies to restore balance, ease tension, and support your wellbeing.

Reflexology

Reflexology is a therapeutic technique that applies targeted pressure to specific points on the feet, hands, and ears. These points correspond to different organs and systems in the body.

By stimulating these reflex points, we help promote natural healing, improve circulation, and encourage deep relaxation. Many clients find it particularly beneficial for stress, headaches, and sleep difficulties.

Acupuncture

Acupuncture is an ancient practice that uses fine needles placed at specific points on the body to restore balance and stimulate healing. It is widely recognised for its effectiveness in treating pain, stress, and a range of health conditions.

Our qualified practitioners create a calm, safe environment where you can experience the benefits of this powerful therapy. Many clients report feeling deeply relaxed and restored after a session.

50 or 60 min Pain & stress relief 1-to-1 session
Book Acupuncture

Swedish Massage

Swedish massage uses long, flowing strokes, kneading, and gentle pressure to ease tension, improve circulation, and promote overall relaxation. Each session is tailored to your individual needs.

Whether you're carrying tension from daily life, dealing with muscle stiffness, or simply need time to unwind, Swedish massage can help ease discomfort and restore a sense of calm.
