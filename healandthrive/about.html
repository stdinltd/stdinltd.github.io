About Us

A community-led organisation supporting people through holistic therapies in Belfast.

Our Mission

Heal & Thrive CIC is a community-led organisation created to support people in real distress through access to holistic therapies such as reflexology, acupuncture, and Swedish massage.

Every week, we meet people in our community who are carrying heavy emotional and physical burdens -grief, trauma, anxiety, burnout, and isolation. Many are already at breaking point, and for them, simple, compassionate touch and holistic support can be the first moment of relief they've felt in a long time. It can be the moment they breathe again.

We're committed to ensuring that people who may never normally have access to holistic therapies can experience them in a safe, trauma-informed, and dignified way. We are in partnerships with community centres in the Colin area -this has shown us just how powerful and necessary this work is, and, importantly, how much more is still needed.

There is so much healing happening at Heal & Thrive Wellness Hub. Every day, we see the positive impact our work is having on the people who walk through our doors -creating a safe, welcoming space where people can pause, reconnect, restore, and begin to heal. The stories, changes, and moments of hope we witness remind us just how important this work is and the difference it can make in people's lives.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do.

Compassion

We meet every person with kindness and empathy, creating a space where you feel truly welcome and understood.

Community

As a CIC, we exist to serve our community -partnering with community centres in the Colin area and ensuring holistic therapies are accessible to those who need them most.

Accessibility

We believe wellness should be available to everyone. We strive to keep our services affordable and our space welcoming to all.

Our Space

Step into Heal & Thrive Wellness Hub and feel the difference. Our space has been carefully designed with your comfort in mind -from the warm wooden panelling to the calming sage green tones throughout.

Every detail has been considered to create an environment where you can truly relax, from the natural materials to the soft lighting and peaceful atmosphere.

Whether you're receiving reflexology, acupuncture, or a Swedish massage, we want you to feel safe, welcome, and at home.
