About Heal & Thrive

A Community Interest Company dedicated to accessible wellness in Belfast.

Our Mission

Heal & Thrive was founded with a simple belief: that everyone deserves access to care that supports their mental and physical wellbeing.

As a Community Interest Company, our purpose goes beyond profit. Every service we provide contributes to our community, and we extend our reach through talks, outreach, and connecting with local mental health organisations.

We believe that healing happens in a space of trust, warmth, and genuine care. That's what we strive to create - for every person who walks through our door.

Our Values

The principles that guide everything we do.

Compassion

We meet every person with kindness and empathy, creating a space where you feel truly welcome and understood.

Community

As a CIC, we exist to serve our community through wellness services, talks, and connecting with local mental health organisations.

Accessibility

We believe wellness should be available to everyone. We strive to keep our services affordable and our space welcoming to all.

Our Space

Step into Heal & Thrive and feel the difference. Our space has been carefully designed with your comfort in mind - from the warm wooden panelling to the calming sage green tones throughout.

Every detail has been considered to create an environment where you can truly relax, from the natural materials to the soft lighting and peaceful atmosphere.

Whether you're attending a yoga class, receiving a massage, or simply sitting in our waiting area with a cup of herbal tea, we want you to feel at home.
