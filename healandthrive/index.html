A Space for Healing

Heal & Thrive CIC is a community-led organisation created to support people in real distress through access to holistic therapies such as reflexology, acupuncture, and Swedish massage.

We're committed to ensuring that people who may never normally have access to holistic therapies can experience them in a safe, trauma-informed, and dignified way.

There is so much healing happening at Heal & Thrive Wellness Hub. Every day, we see the positive impact our work is having on the people who walk through our doors.
