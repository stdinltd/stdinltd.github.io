A Space for Healing

Heal & Thrive is a Community Interest Company in Belfast, created to provide accessible wellness services in a calm, nurturing environment.

Our space features warm wooden panelling and calming sage tones, designed to put you at ease from the moment you walk through the door.

As a CIC, we're committed to promoting wellbeing in our community through talks, outreach, and connecting with local mental health organisations.
